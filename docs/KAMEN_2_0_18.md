# KAMEN 2.0.18

## Song-specific production and raster feedback

The final editorial pass no longer gives every song the same nine-shot sequence. It reads the audio-derived flow/punch/drive/drift profile, selected style, lyric meaning, repetition phase, and measured artwork regions. Calm songs use different compositions and retain source color in their climax; saturated poster treatments derive hue from actual artwork pixels instead of forcing every artwork to blue; punch and drive use different generic-lyric views and motion strengths. Measured top-three styles now have visibly different narrative variants (horizontal/vertical split and opposing negative-space portraits). User-locked cuts remain authoritative.

Production feedback renders RGB and actual lyric alpha at three interior times per phrase plus four opening times. Adjacent luminance-layout signatures and within-phrase RGB changes detect stagnation and motion deficits. Semantic character motion is compared against a disabled-motion control with neutral foreground layers. Two bounded variants alter composition, foreground development and camera movement. Only the variant with a better measured rank survives; LRC timestamps and lyric content are untouched. This is sparse pre-encode raster evidence, not optical flow, object recognition, decoded MP4 verification, linguistic understanding or human artistic judgment. Existing novelty thresholds remain unchanged.

Voice retains monochrome with black negative space and gains a slow push and text breathing. Grounded unity gains a restrained rise in scale. Repetitions add moving foreground layers, with song-specific presentation. Climax entry no longer immediately applies the last phrase's deepest crop; phase and motion continue to grow. The final .45 seconds contract the final lyric to a compact resolution while the background returns to the beginning. Title and lyric never crossfade.

## Honest QA and export profiles

Visual World and the audience-impact proxy cannot remain at 100 while perceptual novelty or temporal contrast is below 80 or unmeasured. These caps are additional to the previous per-domain dependencies. Missing semantic/motion/beat measurements still prohibit certification. Direction-realization findings remain in QA and request the actual raster repair path; generic metadata repair preserves already measured style choices. Body lyric ornaments are not added just to satisfy a signature score; missing style cues remain reported. Storyboards are updated to the selected repaired raster state. Residual motion and stagnation failures remain visible after the bounded search.

Social evidence explicitly distinguishes full-MV suitability from a separately rendered 12–15 second portrait re-edit. A full landscape video does not establish a Short score. UI and Director Context name this distinction; provenance includes the scope, raster feedback and separate loop image/text/audio components. Loop quality includes the actual full-frame text seam instead of reporting background continuity alone.

New project default and automatic preset use 1080p/30fps. Automatic single-artwork FPS prefers 30. Explicit 60fps and the explicitly labeled X lightweight 720p preset remain available. A 1080p delivery preset with a sub-1080 output is an export-blocking profile mismatch.

## Validation

Same-source fixtures for four musical profiles are checked for different actual production pixels, not just different shot names. A forced frozen renderer must be detected despite its metadata. Repair must retain a non-worse rank and preserve full lyric onsets. Existing multi-aspect, range loop, glyph, source/provenance, renderer and export checks remain in the suite. Browser and actual encoded MP4 checks require their own runtime and are not implied by Node raster tests.
