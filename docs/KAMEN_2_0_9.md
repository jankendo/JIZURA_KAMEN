# KAMEN 2.0.9 — rendered lyric selection

The supplied 2.0.8 export is not an artistic 100-point result. The final photo wrapper reduced registered styles to three layouts and one font. A repaired global zoom smeared already readable lyrics. Some native layouts add unrelated text or duplicate the lyric around the frame.

The new automatic route compares all 24 styles using the actual phrases, executes every lyric layout, tests 102 entrances and 38 holds, then selects one composition plan. It rejects missing/replaced/extra text, clipped or displaced phrases, overlapping glyph boxes, extreme glyph stretching, insufficient opacity, and unstable fragments. The thematic layout policy remains explicit. Repeated phrases and adjacent compositions compete with penalties for repeated families. Preview, social re-edit, preflight, export and saved provenance consume the same persisted recipes. Original audio, lyric content and cue times are preserved.

Native dispatch evidence covers 24 styles and 724 techniques: two aspects, three phases, 4,476 samples and 8,952 renderer calls including primary-lyric ablation. Credit and instrumental layouts are explicitly excluded from lyric tests. Execution is not artistic approval. Per-project comparisons and per-ID dispatch evidence are included in the analysis pack context.

Automatic post effects overlapping an unlocked lyric are removed before repair and guarded during rendering. Manual effects and explicit direction remain. Rendering restores the plan's event arrays. An absent event is marked inapplicable, with a null score, rather than rated as either a failed effect or a perfect effect. Legacy repairs cannot overwrite the measured selection with a fresh unmeasured proposal.

The attached source reconstruction passes 120 onset/settled checks across 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and 4:5. Additional tests cover phrase preservation, actual reprise-mask differences, source immutability, one candidate, progress completion, selection persistence through UI-style preflight and social re-edit, invalidation after changed lyric/cue/background settings, and manual intent.

Regression: 158 passed; two browser checks unavailable (variable-font verification and browser MP4 export/reload). Native Canvas uses local fallback fonts and does not prove the appearance of every remotely loaded font. Low-resolution probes, LRC timing and pixel differences do not establish singing alignment, artistic perfection or viral audience response. Browser export settings, frame timestamps and PCM quality were not reduced.
